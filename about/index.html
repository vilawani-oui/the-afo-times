About

A newspaper that keeps its own time.

The Afo Times publishes on the Igbo market week, Eke, Orie, Afo, Nkwo, so an issue lands every Afo day: every fourth day, 91 times a year.

47 cities, 28 desks

Our map runs from Lagos, Asaba and Benin City to Accra, Dakar and Kinshasa; from Athens, Paris and London to Washington, DC, Kingston and São Paulo: 47 cities where the African and Mediterranean diasporas live, work and argue. Every story is filed to one of 28 desks, from Power and Economy to Food, Style, Books, Language and the Afro-Atlantic.

The Afro-Atlantic desk is the beat no other paper has. It is what ties Lagos, DC, Paris and Athens into one readership instead of four unrelated bureaus.

The rhythm

At the end of each year, the editions are collected as an ebook, an audiobook and a printed book.

What you can read now

Until our reporting begins with the first full treizaine of January 2027 (January 6 to 18), the front page gathers headlines from newsrooms in our cities. Each one opens on Rudo and links straight to the publisher who reported it. Our originals and classics are on Rudo in full.
